# Code of Conduct

NextNTech.org exists so that every curious kid can learn technology. Our community should
reflect that: welcoming, patient, and safe for everyone — including young people who may
be contributing to an open-source project for the first time.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make taking part in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level
of experience, education, nationality, language, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, especially with beginners. Everyone was new once.
- Explain rather than judge. "Here's why this could break" beats "this is wrong".
- Respect every language community. English, Dari and Pashto speakers are equal here.
- Accept feedback gracefully, and give it constructively.
- Focus on what's best for the learners.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, of any kind, anywhere in the project.
- Contacting other contributors privately, especially minors, for anything unrelated to
  the project, or asking anyone for personal information.
- Publishing anyone's private information without explicit permission.
- Trolling, spam, or deliberately disrupting discussions.

## Young contributors

Some contributors may be under 18. Keep all project communication in public GitHub
threads. Never move conversations with a young contributor to private channels.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues and pull requests that
break this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behavior is harmful.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer, [@PDostiyar](https://github.com/PDostiyar),
through GitHub. For anything involving the safety of a child, use the private reporting
route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports are handled confidentially.
